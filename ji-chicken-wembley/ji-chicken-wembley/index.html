Where it started

From Taipei's night markets to Olympic Way

Founder Roger Jen left Taiwan for the UK, and kept missing the food more than anywhere else back home. JI Chicken started as an attempt to bring that exact night-market plate — hot, cracklingly crisp, tossed in front of you — to London.

What began as one counter in Chinatown grew into a small UK-wide family of branches, but the recipe hasn't changed: fresh-fried to order, and finished with whichever of the house seasoning powders you're in the mood for.
